Logo: clear space and misuse

A crescent moon cradling a closed bud. Use the files in brand/logo as they are.

Clear space = the height of the bud

Keep the shaded area free of text, edges and other logos. The darker squares in the corners are one unit: the bud's height. It scales with the logo.

Mark. Clear space on every side equals the bud's height.

Horizontal lockup. The same unit around the whole lockup. Mark-to-wordmark spacing is fixed; do not change it.

Minimum size

Shown at actual size. Below these sizes use the mark alone.

Do not

Examples of misuse, shown so they can be recognised. Never publish these.

  • Recolour the logo outside the four variants.

  • Stretch, squash or skew it.

  • Add glows, shadows or gradients.

  • Place it on the horizon gradient or a busy photo. Use a solid surface.

  • Rotate it or rearrange the mark and wordmark.

  • AI

    Add sparkles, extra stars or "AI" badges around it.
