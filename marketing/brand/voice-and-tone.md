# Moonbloom Labs voice and tone

Status: draft for owner approval. Applies to every channel and both launch languages (EN, SK). Source: `CLAUDE.md` brand guardrails and `docs/plan/copy.md` §0.2.

## 1. Who we sound like

A senior architect who is also a good host: calm, warm, precise and a little playful. We explain what happens and what does not. We never shout, hype or pressure.

| We are                                                                   | We are not                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Calm.** Short sentences, no exclamation marks.                         | Loud, urgent, breathless                  |
| **Warm.** We talk to one person, about their idea.                       | Corporate, distant, jargon-first          |
| **Precise.** We say exactly what arrives in the morning.                 | Vague ("magic", "seamless", "next-level") |
| **Playful, lightly.** The night-and-bloom metaphor, used with restraint. | Pun-heavy, cute, emoji-laden              |
| **Honest.** A prototype is a prototype; we say what is still open.       | Overpromising, absolute claims            |

## 2. Principles

1. **Say what "done" means.** Whenever we say "done", "ready" or "by morning", name the deliverables nearby: a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and open decisions.
2. **A human decides; agents build.** Make the human role explicit. Never imply that nobody checks the work, and never claim a person watches the build all night.
3. **Show, don't brag.** No superlatives, no "best", no "world-class". Facts about the process beat adjectives.
4. **Never invent proof.** No clients, logos, testimonials, case studies, metrics or prices unless the owner has confirmed them. Use a visible `[TODO: ...]` until then.
5. **One metaphor, used well.** Plant, bloom, night, morning. Do not mix in rockets, rocket fuel, superpowers or magic.
6. **Respect the reader's time.** Lead with the outcome, then the how. One idea per post, one ask per email.

## 3. Vocabulary

**Use:** plant, bloom, overnight, evening, night, morning, ready, done, prototype, decide, approve, refine, open decisions, path to production, a human decides, agents build.

**Avoid (soft):** magic, seamless, effortless, game-changing, disrupt, supercharge, unleash, 10x, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, AI-powered as a lone selling point, rockstar, ninja.

**Never use (hard ban, any language, any casing):**

| EN                    | SK                    |
| --------------------- | --------------------- |
| grind                 | drina                 |
| hustle                | makať                 |
| 24/7                  | 24/7                  |
| nonstop               | nonstop               |
| night shift           | nočná zmena           |
| revolutionary         | revolučný             |
| guarantee, guaranteed | garancia, garantujeme |

Why: the brand is about calm overnight work done by agents with human judgement, not about people working through the night or promises nobody can keep.

## 4. Do and don't

| Don't                                                     | Do                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Revolutionary AI that builds your app while you sleep!!" | "Plant an idea at night. Wake up to a working prototype, its tests and a short demo."       |
| "Guaranteed results by morning."                          | "We agree the scope in the evening so it fits one night."                                   |
| "Our AI does everything."                                 | "AI agents build. A senior architect plans and reviews. You decide."                        |
| "Trusted by leading companies."                           | (Say nothing until a real, approved client exists.)                                         |
| "Production-ready in 12 hours."                           | "A prototype, not a production system. When you approve one, we can take it to production." |
| "Don't miss out, book now!"                               | "Have an idea for tonight? Tell us about it."                                               |

## 5. Formatting

- Sentence case for every headline, button and subject line. No all-caps.
- No exclamation marks. At most one emoji per social post, and only from the brand's world (🌙, 🌱, 🌸), never ✨ or 🚀 or 🤖.
- Numbers: "20+ years" is a confirmed fact about the founder; do not add other numbers without a source.
- Oxford comma in EN. Dates as "10 November 2026".
- Links: describe the destination ("See how it works"), never "click here".
- Hashtags: two at most, at the end (#AIprototyping, #MoonbloomLabs).

## 6. Slovak

- Formal address (vykanie) with lowercase "vy, váš" on the web and in marketing.
- Put a non-breaking space after one-letter prepositions and conjunctions (a, i, k, o, s, u, v, z).
- Do not translate word for word. Adapt the bloom metaphor so it sounds natural; "Ráno je hotovo." rather than a literal "Zobuďte sa do hotového".
- Every Slovak text is a **draft until a native speaker reviews it**. Mark files accordingly.

## 7. Tone by channel

| Channel                | Tone shift                                                                                |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Website                | The reference voice.                                                                      |
| LinkedIn (company)     | Clear and informative; one concrete idea per post.                                        |
| LinkedIn (founder)     | First person, reflective, practical lessons from building.                                |
| X / Bluesky            | Shortest form; one sentence that stands alone.                                            |
| Email                  | Personal, short, one ask. Signed by a person.                                             |
| Press                  | Neutral third person, facts only, no metaphors in the first paragraph except the tagline. |
| Sales deck / one-pager | Precise: process, deliverables, next step.                                                |
