## 1. Roof: the brand promise

> **Ideas bloom overnight.**
> Plant an idea at night. Wake up to done: a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and the decisions that are yours to make.

## 2. Positioning statement

**For** product owners, innovation leads, CTOs and founders **who** need to know quickly whether an AI idea works, **Moonbloom Labs is** an AI studio for working prototypes **that** turns an idea described in the evening into a tested prototype by the next morning. **Unlike** agencies that take weeks to deliver a first version, or do-it-yourself AI tools that leave quality and judgement to you, **Moonbloom** pairs AI agents that build overnight with a senior architect who plans and reviews every build, and leaves every decision that matters with people.

## 6. Elevator pitches

**10 seconds.** Moonbloom Labs turns your idea into a working AI prototype by the next morning. You plant it at night; you wake up to done.

**30 seconds.** Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio for working prototypes. In the evening you describe your idea in a short call, and we agree a scope that fits one night. AI agents build it overnight, following a plan a senior architect has reviewed. In the morning you get a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and a short list of decisions only you can make. Then you decide: approve it, refine it, or plan the path to production.

**60 seconds.** Waiting weeks to find out whether an idea works is often the slowest part of a project. AI agents can now write and test a lot of code in one night, but they need direction and judgement. Moonbloom Labs puts the two together. Our founder, an AI and cloud architect with more than 20 years in cloud, DevOps and AI/ML, plans and reviews every build. You tell us your idea in the evening; we cut it to a scope that fits one night; agents build and test it; and in the morning you review a working prototype, its tests, a demo and the open decisions. We build RAG search, knowledge bases, AI assistants, gamification and AI integrations, and when a prototype earns its place, our DevOps and cloud work takes it to production. A human decides; agents build.
