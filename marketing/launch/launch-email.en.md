# Launch email (EN)

Status: draft for owner approval. Send only to people the founder knows personally or who have opted in (GDPR). Send individually or through a mail tool with an unsubscribe link; never with a visible list of recipients.

**Subject options**

1. Ideas bloom overnight: I've started Moonbloom Labs
2. Have an idea for tonight?
3. A working prototype by tomorrow morning

**Preview text:** Plant an idea at night. Wake up to a working prototype, its tests and a short demo.

---

## Plain text

Hi {first name},

I've started something new and wanted you to hear it from me first.

Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio for working prototypes. You tell me your idea in a short evening call, and we agree a scope that fits one night. AI agents build it overnight, following a plan I've reviewed. In the morning you get:

- a working prototype you can click through or try,
- its automated tests,
- a short demo,
- a short list of decisions only you can make.

Then you decide: approve it, refine it over another night, or plan the path to production.

It's a prototype, not a finished product, and I'll always say what fits into one night and what doesn't. A human decides; agents build.

We build RAG search over company documents, knowledge bases, AI assistants, gamification, AI integrations, and the DevOps and cloud work to take a good prototype to production.

If there's an idea waiting on your list, or someone in your team who has one, I'd love to hear it: https://moonbloomlabs.com/contact/

Or just reply to this email.

Thank you,
Michal

Michal Maxian
Founder, Moonbloom Labs
https://moonbloomlabs.com
