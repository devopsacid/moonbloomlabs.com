## Moonbloom Labs launches an AI studio that turns ideas into working prototypes overnight

_Describe an idea in the evening; review a working, tested prototype the next morning. A senior architect plans and reviews every build, and every decision stays with people._

**Bratislava, [TODO: release date]** — Moonbloom Labs today launched an AI studio for working prototypes. Clients describe an idea in a short evening call, AI agents build it overnight following a plan reviewed by a senior architect, and the next morning the client receives a working prototype, its automated tests, a short demo and a list of open decisions only they can make.

Many projects spend weeks before anyone can see whether an idea works. Moonbloom Labs shortens that first step to a single night, while keeping human judgement around the parts that matter: the scope is agreed with the client in the evening, the build follows a reviewed plan, and nothing ships without human approval.

"Waiting weeks to find out whether an idea works is often the slowest part of a project," said Michal Maxian, founder of Moonbloom Labs. "AI agents can now write and test a lot of code in one night. What they need is direction and judgement. At Moonbloom a human decides and agents build, so you wake up to something you can actually try, and to the questions only you can answer."

Moonbloom Labs builds prototypes in six areas:

- **RAG & AI search:** ask company documents questions and get answers with citations to the source.
- **Knowledge management:** one searchable, maintained knowledge base from scattered docs, wikis and chats.
- **Chatbots & AI assistants:** assistants for customer support, internal helpdesks and onboarding.
- **Gamification:** game mechanics for learning, onboarding, engagement and financial literacy.
- **AI integration:** language models and agents connected to existing systems, APIs and workflows.
- **DevOps & cloud:** CI/CD, Kubernetes, AWS, infrastructure as code and observability, so approved prototypes can become production systems.

The studio is honest about what one night can do: it delivers a prototype, not a finished product. After the morning review, clients decide whether to approve the prototype, refine it over another night, or plan the path to production. Clients own the prototype code and documentation [TODO: confirm IP terms]. Moonbloom Labs can sign an NDA before an idea is discussed in detail, builds with sample or anonymised data where possible, and agrees up front which AI services may process client data.

Moonbloom Labs works in English and Slovak. Ideas can be submitted at **moonbloomlabs.com**.
