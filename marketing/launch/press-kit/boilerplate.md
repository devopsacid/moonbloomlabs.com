## EN

**25 words**
Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio that turns an idea into a working prototype by the next morning. A human decides; agents build. Ideas bloom overnight.

**50 words**
Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio for working prototypes. Clients describe an idea in the evening; AI agents build and test it overnight under a plan a senior architect has reviewed. In the morning the client reviews a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and the decisions only they can make.

**100 words**
Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio that turns an idea into a working prototype by the next morning. Clients describe an idea in a short evening call and agree a scope that fits one night. AI agents build and test it overnight, following a plan reviewed by a senior architect. In the morning the client receives a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and a list of open decisions. Founded by AI and cloud architect Michal Maxian, the studio builds RAG search, knowledge bases, AI assistants, gamification and AI integrations, and takes approved prototypes to production. https://moonbloomlabs.com
