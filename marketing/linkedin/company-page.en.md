## Overview / About (≤2,000)

> Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio for working prototypes.
>
> Plant an idea at night. Wake up to done.
>
> In the evening you describe your idea in a short call or a written brief, and together we cut it to a scope that fits one night. AI agents build it overnight, following a plan a senior architect has reviewed. In the morning you get:
>
> → a working prototype you can click through, call or try
> → its automated tests
> → a short demo
> → a short list of open decisions only you can make
>
> Then you decide: approve it, refine it over another night, or plan the path to production.
>
> A human decides; agents build. Nothing ships without human approval. Every build is planned and reviewed by an AI and cloud architect with more than 20 years in cloud, DevOps and AI/ML.
>
> What we build:
> · RAG & AI search: ask your documents questions, get answers that cite the source
> · Knowledge management: one searchable, maintained knowledge base
> · Chatbots & AI assistants: support, internal helpdesk, onboarding
> · Gamification: game mechanics for learning, onboarding and engagement
> · AI integration: language models and agents connected to your systems
> · DevOps & cloud: CI/CD, Kubernetes, AWS, infrastructure as code, observability
>
> A prototype is not a production system, and we say so. We tell you in the evening what fits, and in the morning what is still missing. We can sign an NDA, build with sample or anonymised data, and agree up front which AI services may process it.
>
> We work in English and Slovak.
>
> Have an idea for tonight? moonbloomlabs.com
