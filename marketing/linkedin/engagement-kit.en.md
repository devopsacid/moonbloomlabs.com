# LinkedIn engagement kit (EN)

Status: draft for owner approval. Short, personal, never automated. LinkedIn penalises copy-pasted mass messages, and so do people. Change at least one line every time.

## 1. Connection request notes (≤200 characters for free accounts; counts in brackets)

- **Someone you've worked with:** "Hi {name}, it's been a while since {project}. I've just started Moonbloom Labs, AI prototypes overnight, and would love to stay in touch here." (≈150)
- **Someone who engaged with a post:** "Hi {name}, thanks for your comment on my post about {topic}. Happy to connect." (≈90)
- **Someone you met at an event:** "Hi {name}, good to meet you at {event}. Let's keep in touch here." (≈75)
- Never put a sales pitch into a connection request.

## 2. Direct-message replies

**They're interested ("How does this work for us?")**

> Thanks, {name}. The short version: you describe the idea in the evening, we agree a scope that fits one night, and in the morning you get a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and the open decisions. Would a 20-minute call this week help? If you prefer writing, send me a few sentences on what you'd want to see working: moonbloomlabs.com/contact/

**They ask about price**

> Good question. [TODO: pricing]. Until pricing is set: "It depends on the scope we agree in the evening, and you get a fixed figure before anything is built. Happy to give you one for your idea." [TODO: owner to confirm this wording]

**"Not now"**

> Completely fine, thanks for letting me know. If an idea comes up later, you know where to find me.

**They congratulate you**

> Thank you, {name}, that means a lot. If you ever have an idea you'd like to see working by morning, you know who to ask.

**A recruiter or vendor pitch:** ignore or decline politely. No templates needed.

## 3. Comment-reply bank

Reply to every comment within the first hour of a post if you can. Replies count as engagement, and people notice them.

| Comment type                   | Reply                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Congrats" / "Great idea"      | "Thank you, {name}. What would you plant first?"                                                                                                                 |
| "Overnight? Really?"           | "Fair question. One night builds a prototype, not a product: one clear idea with a core flow you can try. The evening scope call is what makes it fit."          |
| "Who checks the AI's work?"    | "People, at both ends. I plan and review every build; you approve the scope in the evening and the result in the morning. Nothing ships without human approval." |
| "What about data privacy?"     | "We can sign an NDA, use sample or anonymised data, and agree up front which AI services may process it."                                                        |
| "How much?"                    | Move it to a DM: "Depends on the scope. I'll send you a message."                                                                                                |
| "Can you do X?"                | "Possibly. Send me two sentences on what you'd want working, and I'll tell you honestly whether it fits into one night."                                         |
| Criticism of AI-built software | "Agreed that unchecked AI code is risky. That's why every build follows a reviewed plan, ships with tests, and ends with a list of decisions for a person."      |

## 4. Reshare copy for friends and colleagues (advocacy)

Send privately to 10–20 people who know the founder well, on launch morning, with a link to the founder's launch post. Ask, don't expect.

**Message to them:**

> Hi {name}, today I'm launching Moonbloom Labs. If you're up for it, a like, comment or reshare of this post would help a lot in the first hours: {link}. A few lines you can use or ignore are below. Thank you.

**Lines they can use when resharing:**

- "I've worked with Michal for {years}. If you have an AI idea waiting for a proof of concept, this is worth a look."
- "Plant an idea in the evening, wake up to a working prototype with tests. Congrats on the launch, Michal."
- "A human decides, agents build. I like this approach to AI prototyping. Congrats, Michal."
- (SK) „Nápad večer, funkčný prototyp ráno. Gratulujem k spusteniu, Michal.“

## 5. Inviting people to follow the company page

LinkedIn gives the page a monthly allowance of follow invites. Use them after launch day, on people who reacted to the launch posts first, then on the founder's closest network. No message is attached; the page's tagline does the work.

## 6. Hashtags

At most three per post, at the end:

- Always: #AIprototyping
- Rotate: #AIagents · #GenerativeAI · #RAG · #DevOps · #Gamification · #ProofOfConcept
- Brand (company page posts only): #MoonbloomLabs
- SK posts: #AIprototyp #MoonbloomLabs

## 7. Etiquette

- Post the link in the first comment, not in the post body.
- Don't tag people who haven't agreed to it. Never tag companies as if they were clients.
- Don't use engagement pods or bought followers.
- Answer DMs within one working day.
