## P09 · L+4 · MM · text · Behind the night

> People ask what actually happens between the evening call and the morning delivery. Here's the honest version.
>
> Before the night starts, the idea becomes a written plan: what to build, what "working" means, what is out of scope, and how it will be tested. I review that plan. Only then do the agents start.
>
> Overnight, AI agents write the code and the tests together. Tests aren't an extra; they are how the agents, and later you, know what actually works.
>
> In the morning, before anything reaches you, the result is checked against the plan. Whatever the agents couldn't decide on their own goes on a short list: the open decisions.
>
> Nobody watches the build all night. That's the point of a good plan and good tests.
