Moonbloom Labs is an AI studio that turns an idea into a working prototype by the next morning. AI agents do the building; a person sets the direction and approves every step.

A prototype, not a production system. We agree the scope in the evening so it fits one night.

From evening idea to morning prototype

  1. Evening

    You describe the idea

    In a short call or a written brief you tell us what you want to see working. Together we cut it to a scope that fits one night.

  2. Night

    Agents build and test

    AI agents write the code and its tests, following the plan a senior architect reviewed before the build started.

  3. Morning

    Prototype, tests, demo, questions

    You receive a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and a list of open questions and decisions.

  4. Decision

    Approve or refine

    You decide what happens next: approve it, refine it in another night, or plan the path to production.
