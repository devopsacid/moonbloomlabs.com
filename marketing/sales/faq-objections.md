## Qualifying questions for the intake call

1. What should exist tomorrow morning that doesn't exist today?
2. Who will use it, and what does "working" mean to them?
3. What's the one question this prototype must answer?
4. What sample data or access can we use? Any data that must not leave your environment?
5. Which AI services are allowed to process the data?
6. Who decides in the morning, and when will they look at it?
7. If it works, what happens next: demo, pilot, production?
