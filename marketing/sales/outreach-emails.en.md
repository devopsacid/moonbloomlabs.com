# Outreach emails (EN)

Status: draft for owner approval. Rules: one person, one idea, one ask. Write only to people with a real connection or a legitimate business reason (GDPR, B2B legitimate interest); offer an easy opt-out; never send in bulk. Replace every `{…}` before sending.

---

## 1. Warm intro (someone you know)

**Subject:** An idea for {company}, by tomorrow morning

Hi {first name},

I've started Moonbloom Labs, an AI studio for working prototypes. You describe an idea in the evening; AI agents build and test it overnight, following a plan I review; in the morning you get a working prototype, its tests, a short demo and the decisions only you can make.

When we last spoke you mentioned {specific problem or idea}. That sounds like something one night could answer: {one-line prototype suggestion}.

Would a 20-minute call next week be useful to see whether it fits?

Michal

## 3. Considered cold note (legitimate business interest, researched)

**Subject:** {their initiative}: a working prototype first?

Hi {first name},

I read about {their initiative, with source}. Before a project like that gets planned in detail, it often helps to see the core idea working.

That's what we do at Moonbloom Labs: in the evening we agree a scope that fits one night; AI agents build and test it overnight under a plan I review; in the morning you try a working prototype and get a short list of open decisions. It's a prototype, not a finished product, and we're precise about that.

If it's relevant, I'd be glad to hear what you'd want to see working. If not, no reply needed and I won't follow up more than once.

Michal Maxian
Founder, Moonbloom Labs · moonbloomlabs.com
If you'd rather not hear from me, reply "no thanks" and I'll remove your details.
